author Jonah Ostroff
run_rules_on_level_start
Background_Color #180018
realtime_interval 5

(
Every 5 seconds, a click happens:
Runner gets 1 credit.
Corp gets 1 credit per installed agenda.
Installed agendas advance and possibly score, activating their subroutines.
Subroutines take effect.

Corp uses W/S to select agenda or ice, which agenda or ice, and which server to install on. Install cost is always 1.

When you run, you go through ice in order, and the following things happen:
A: If the ice is unrezzed, the corp rezzes it by paying its strength, if possible. If they cannot afford this, it remains unrezzed and the runner moves on.
B: If the ice is rezzed, the runner compares his credits to the strength of the ice, and compares his breaker color to the ice.
• If the credits are sufficient and the breakers match, the credits are spent, the ice is destroyed, and the runner moves on.
• If the credits are sufficient and the breakers don't match, the credits are spent and the ice's strength is reduced by 1, to a minimum of 1, and the runner moves on.
• If the credits are insufficient and the breakers match, the runner bypasses the ice for free.
• If the credits are insufficient and the breakers don't match, the run ends and the subroutine is activated, taking effect at the next click.
C: If an agenda is encountered, the runner steals the agenda and moves on.
D: If the end of the server is reached, the runner's breaker changes to that server's color and the run ends.

After each run, the corp gains 1 credit.

Subroutines:
Red (Jinteki?) spawns strength 1 ice
Green (Weyland?) increases the strength of a random rezzed ice.
Blue (Haas-Bioroid?) prevents the runner from running and gaining credits for 1 tick.
Yellow (NBN) sets the corp's credit pool to 5.

Ice selector always shows two agendas in the top row (W) and two ice in the bottom row (S). The cheaper ice is always on the left.

Advance agenda 5 times (20-25 seconds) to score, access to steal.
Scored (not stolen) agendas trigger the subroutine of their color.

)
