LEGEND
=======

. = Background
# = Wall
P = Player
+ = Player and Target
* = Crate
@ = Crate and Target
O = Target
% = Player and Stopper
x = Stopper
- = Player and Movestop
8 = Crate and Movestop
s = Movestop

Movecheck = MovecheckL or MovecheckR or MovecheckU or MovecheckD
Blocking = Crate or Wall or Stopper
SightBlocking = Crate or Wall or Movestop

======
RULES
======

(I tried to place a [...] in the basic push and ended up with something cool. I tried to ajust the code and ended up with something like this.)

late left [Player | no Movecheck no SightBlocking] -> [Player | MovecheckL]
late right [Player | no Movecheck no SightBlocking] -> [Player | MovecheckR]
late up [Player | no Movecheck no SightBlocking] -> [Player | MovecheckU]
late down [Player | no Movecheck no SightBlocking] -> [Player | MovecheckD]

late left [MovecheckL | no Movecheck no SightBlocking] -> [MovecheckL | MovecheckL]
late right [MovecheckR | no Movecheck no SightBlocking] -> [MovecheckR | MovecheckR]
late up [MovecheckU | no Movecheck no SightBlocking] -> [MovecheckU | MovecheckU]
late down [MovecheckD | no Movecheck no SightBlocking] -> [MovecheckD | MovecheckD]

(This code generate 4 lines of Movechecks (one of each direction). These are blocked by a group called "SightBlocking")

[> Player | ... | Movecheck | Crate] -> [Player | ... | Movecheck | > Crate]
[> Player | Crate] -> [> Player | > Crate]
[> Crate | Blocking] -> [Crate | Blocking] sfx0

[Movecheck] -> []

(Bottom code is the easiest to understand. Keep in mind that rule 149 and rule 150 are completly different.)
(Rule 155 is just for clearing)
